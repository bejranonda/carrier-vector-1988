## 2. Still weak

1. **No human has played it — and for this audience that matters most.** A
   bot does not get confused by a word, tired eyes, or a trackpad. The next
   round must include players over 50, on their own devices.
2. **Phones cannot enlarge text (#103).** AARP's 84%-on-smartphone figure
   makes this the biggest remaining gap for older players.
3. **Small labels are still small (#104).** 10 px becomes 15 px at EXTRA
   LARGE; the Xbox guideline asks 28 px at 1080p as a floor. A zoom cannot get
   there without breaking layouts — it needs fewer, larger labels.
4. **EASY is one preset.** The guidelines ask for four difficulty levels or
   individual assists. EASY bundles speed, damage, jets and aim; a player who
   wants auto-aim but full speed cannot have it.
5. **The adaptive offer is one rule.** Built in the final pass: two jets lost
   in a STANDARD run offers EASY once. It does not yet notice other kinds of
   struggle (a deck mission failed three times, a run with no kills).
6. **Deck missions on EASY still end with a manual trap.** The recovery assist
   hands back at short final. For an older player the canyon strike and
   carrier quals remain hard; EASY lives in SCRAMBLE.
7. **Audio cues are untested by ear**, and there are no captions for the
   spoken-style warnings (they are drawn as text already, but not marked as
   such).
8. **The briefing is still dense** for someone who has never played anything
   (v2.0.0 critique §2.3). The fly-style question now carries a first-timer
   through it, but a one-button first-run briefing would be kinder.
